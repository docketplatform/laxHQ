Who LaxHQ is for

LaxHQ accounts are owned by a parent or guardian. Players may join a family by invitation. LaxHQ isn’t intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address and sign-in method (Google, Apple or email link).
  • Player profile: name, grad year, position, high school, grades and test scores, club team, stats, rankings and film links you enter.
  • Recruiting plan: target schools, notes, coach names and work emails you add, your outreach log, checklist progress and summer events.
  • Club schedule: the calendar link you paste and the events it contains for the calendars you choose. Other calendars in the same feed are discarded.
  • Spending: expenses you enter and receipt images you upload.
  • Recruiting card views: the time of each view and, for links you send to coaches, which school it came from. We don’t store viewers’ IP addresses; we use a one-way hash only to avoid counting repeat views.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe (web) or Apple via RevenueCat (iPhone). We receive your plan status, not your card number.

How we use it

To run the app for your family: build your plan, estimate fit, draft emails you choose to send, check schedule clashes, total your spending and manage your subscription. Anonymous averages (for example, committed families’ GPA at a school) are shown only when you opt in, and only once at least 5 families have shared.

Who we share it with

  • People you choose: anyone you give your recruiting card link to, and family members you invite.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Google Firebase (hosting, database, storage), Netlify (web hosting), Stripe and RevenueCat (billing), and Anthropic (drafting coach emails and reading receipts you upload).
  • Other families: only coach work emails that several families have confirmed, and anonymous averages as described above.

We don’t sell personal information or use it for advertising. LaxHQ never emails coaches on your behalf.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your login, or (as the family’s owner) the whole family and its data, from the Profile tab at any time. Backups are kept for up to 30 days.
