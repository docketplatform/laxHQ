Help

LaxHQ is your family’s college recruiting game plan. Everything below can be done in the app, without contacting anyone.

Getting started

What can I do on the free plan?

Look around everything with a sample family: the Sept 1 countdown, school list, coach pipeline, summer planner and expense tracker. You can fill in your player’s details too; they’re kept in your browser and saved the moment you start Pro.

How do I start Pro?

Profile tab → Start Pro. Choose yearly ($500, with optional siblings at $99/year each) or monthly ($49). Checkout is handled securely by Stripe.

How do I add my spouse or my player?

Profile tab → Invite a parent or Invite your player. Send them the link; it works once and expires after 7 days.

How do I add a sibling?

Siblings need the sibling add-on. On the yearly plan, use Manage billing on the Profile tab to add one, then Profile → Add a sibling.

Using LaxHQ

When can college coaches contact my player?

For Division I lacrosse, coaches can start recruiting conversations on Sept 1 of junior year. Players can email coaches before then. Division II and III rules differ. LaxHQ summarizes the rules for planning; always confirm on ncaa.org.

Does LaxHQ email coaches for me?

No. LaxHQ drafts the email; you review it and send it from your own email account (your email app, Gmail or Outlook). Then tap “I sent it” and LaxHQ reminds you to follow up.

How do I connect my club team’s schedule?

Events tab → Club schedule → Connect, then paste the calendar link from your team app. TeamSnap, SportsEngine, LeagueApps and Google Calendar all offer one; look for “sync”, “subscribe”, “export” or “iCal”. If your feed includes other teams (a sibling’s soccer, for example), pick only the lacrosse calendar.

My club schedule stopped updating.

Team apps sometimes reset calendar links. Copy a fresh link from the team app and connect it again on the Events tab.

Who can see my player’s recruiting card?

Only people you give the link to. The card shows first name and last initial, and you can turn sharing off at any time; old links then show “not available”.

How accurate are the fit estimates and “How you compare”?

They’re estimates from what you enter and from published admissions data for all admitted students. Recruited athletes are often admitted with different numbers, so treat them as a starting point, not a prediction.

Account and billing

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Profile tab → Manage billing opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can switch plans, update your card, download invoices or cancel. If you subscribed in the iPhone app, manage it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data stays. Your account becomes view-only until you start Pro again.

How do I delete my account?

Profile tab → Delete account. The parent who set up the family can delete everything; other members can delete just their own login.
